## Scope

The correspondence covers **Theorem 1.1 and its referenced definitions**, with the closed-body and periodic-covering conventions explained above. The prose-to-formal interpretation is a mathematical explanation; the comparator's Lean checks establish the agreement between the displayed Lean definitions and statements and their proved counterparts.

The target does not link its auxiliary `rho,delta,alpha` to all supplementary texture or reconstruction formulas following Theorem 1.1. It also does not separately assert closed poloidal field lines, quantitative corrections, or every claim in the manuscript. The non-isolation conclusion concerns the moduli space of varying embedded configurations; it is not a fixed-physical-domain assertion.
